Hi there!

I am Yiwei Ding. I'm a PhD student at University of Würzburg in Germany. I work in [Computational Humanities](https://www.caidas.uni-wuerzburg.de/ch/) group at Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (CAIDAS). The group is led by [Prof. Christof Weiß](https://www.caidas.uni-wuerzburg.de/ch/team/christof-weiss/#c1134238).

My research interests include music information retrieval tasks (e.g. chord recognition, key detection, beat tracking, etc.), representation learning for audio and music, and music source separation. Basically, I like all tasks that teaches the computer to listen or to process audio and music. I'm also open and interested to other fields of computational humanities (that I have not worked on yet), putting the traditional humanity topics (e.g. media, movies, society, etc.) under the context of AI.

Before joining University of Würzburg, I was a master's student at Georgia Institute of Technology, majoring in Music Technology. I was doing research in the [Music Informatics Group](https://musicinformatics.gatech.edu/), advised by [Prof. Alexander Lerch](https://www.alexanderlerch.com/). Before that, I completed my bachelor's degree at Fudan University in Shanghai, majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology.
